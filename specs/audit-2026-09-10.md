## Principles, open decisions, and platforms

The active normative principles are `principles/00-index.md`, `agent-loop.md`,
`cognitive-complexity.md`, and `context-hygiene.md`. The newest owner policy controls:
verification is proportionate; optional artifacts are advisory; applicable failures and
contradictory source identity block; stress/load is opt-in; and approved current-head work need
not rebuild solely because its base advanced.

Unresolved without invented approval: immutable evidence export remains held under CG-402
(sanitized access-controlled export versus local-only retention); phase-07 end-to-end evidence
remains held under CG-403/408; and model escalation beyond CG-437's substantive-revision
thresholds remains planned. CG-213 and CG-302 own only the OpenRouter harness and adapter
decision. The focused follow-up filed by CG-505 owns routing retries or revisions to a higher
tier from the specification's remaining objective failure signals, without weakening its
requirement to distinguish implementation failures from environment and infrastructure faults.

Linux, macOS, and Windows through WSL are supported design targets. Portable contracts use
Python, Git, files, subprocesses, HTTPS, and capability checks. Linux-only cgroup/procfs controls
need documented alternatives; macOS has its host-telemetry path; Windows support is through WSL,
not native process semantics. Browser capture can use configured Playwright or local WSL Edge.
This audit validates source and references; it does not claim every platform path was executed.
